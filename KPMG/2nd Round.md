# Interview Questions: 2nd Round

## Questions asked by interviewer

1\. Can you introduce yourself and share anything about your academics, achievements, or experience that would be relevant to this conversation?

2\. Why are you looking for a job change?

3\. Do you currently have any other offers that you are pursuing?

4\. Why are you still looking for a change when you already have two offers? What are those offers missing that you are looking for in this role?

5\. What is your current notice period, and when is your last working day?

6\. Are you pursuing any certifications? I see that you have completed some certifications in Azure and GCP.

7\. You already have two offers. What is it about this KPMG role that interests you and makes you consider joining us?

8\. You understand that this role has a hybrid work setup, with employees expected to come to the office twice a week. Are you comfortable with that arrangement?

## Questions / Clarifications Raised by me

These were questions i asked to the interviewer:

9\. Will this position come under Platform Engineering, and will I work with multiple cross-functional teams on a day-to-day basis, or will I be working closely with a dedicated team?

10\. What industry or business domain does this internal platform security requirement support?

11\. What level of responsibilities and ownership should I expect in this Consultant role?

12\. What are the current challenges the team is facing, and what are you trying to achieve through this role?

13\. Is this a newly created position, or am I being hired as a replacement for someone?

14\. Is there any priority or urgency to close this position?

15\. Is the Platform Security team entirely new, or are there already people working in the team?

## Other Discussion Points

These were statements or explanations rather than direct interview questions:

- The interviewer explained that the role supports KPMG's UK partner firm.
- The team is responsible for end-to-end platform security, including helping design and implement security policies.
- The platform security function is being built from the ground up.
- The role involves establishing security posture for platforms, implementing controls, and ensuring they are followed.
- The interviewer emphasized that candidates receiving an offer should give a clear commitment rather than treating it as an additional option.
