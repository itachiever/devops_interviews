# Answers: 2nd Round

### Question 1: Can you introduce yourself and share anything about your academics, achievements, or experience that would be relevant to this conversation?

Answer:

Good evening, and thank you for the opportunity.

I'm a DevSecOps Engineer with around 4.5 years of overall IT experience. I started my career at PREVIOUS COMPANY, where I worked for approximately 2.5 years as a DevOps Engineer. Later, I transitioned into DevSecOps, and currently I'm working with CURRENT COMPANY, supporting CLIENT, a fintech and payment gateway service provider.

ISG provides B2B payment solutions to banking clients, so application security and regulatory compliance are important aspects of our environment.

We work with a hybrid infrastructure that includes AWS, Azure, and some on-premises deployments. We use GitLab for source-code management and Jenkins for CI/CD orchestration.

My primary responsibility is managing and integrating security tools across the Software Development Life Cycle. Our security stack includes:

- Fortify: SAST and DAST.
- Sonatype Lifecycle: Software Composition Analysis.
- Sonatype SBOM Manager: SBOM and CBOM generation and management.
- Trivy: Container image vulnerability scanning.
- GitLeaks: Secret detection.
- SonarQube: Code-quality analysis.

These tools are integrated into our CI/CD pipelines to identify vulnerabilities early, enforce security checks, and support compliance requirements. I also coordinate with development teams for vulnerability remediation and security improvements.

Regarding achievements, I recently received an Employee of the Quarter award for my contribution towards security standardization and compliance improvements in the fintech project.

Overall, my experience is primarily around DevSecOps, application security, CI/CD security integration, vulnerability management, and automation.

### Question 2: Why are you looking for a job change?

Answer:

There are two main reasons.

First, location is an important factor for me. I'm originally from Andhra Pradesh, and currently I'm working in Mumbai. I'm looking for opportunities in Bangalore because it is more convenient for me geographically and personally.

Second, from a career perspective, I've been working in my current project for around 2.5 years. I've gained good experience in application security, DevSecOps tools, and security integration. Now I'm looking for the next level of professional growth, where I can take on broader responsibilities, work on platform security, and gain deeper hands-on experience in cloud security engineering.

So, I'm looking for a role that offers both a suitable location and an opportunity to grow technically.

### Question 3: Do you currently have any other offers that you are pursuing?

Answer:

Yes, I currently have two offers.

One is from Infosys, and the other is from Persistent Systems.

Both opportunities are relevant to my DevOps and DevSecOps background, but I'm still evaluating the role responsibilities, technical alignment, compensation, and long-term career opportunities before making a final decision.

### Question 4: Why are you still looking for a change when you already have two offers? What are those offers missing that you are looking for in this role?

Answer:

That's a fair question.

Both offers have their own advantages, but I have some differences in terms of role alignment and compensation.

The opportunity from Persistent Systems was quite close to the kind of role I was looking for, particularly from a technical perspective. However, the compensation offered was comparatively lower than my expectations.

With Infosys, the compensation was better, but the role description and responsibilities were not very clear. I tried reaching out to understand the exact project and technical expectations, but I couldn't get sufficient clarity.

What interests me about KPMG is that I have already interacted with a few team members and gained a better understanding of the role.

The Platform Security Consultant position aligns closely with my current DevSecOps experience while also giving me an opportunity to expand into platform engineering and cloud security.

So, I'm not evaluating opportunities based only on compensation. I'm also looking for the right responsibilities, technical growth, and long-term career direction.

### Question 7: You already have two offers. What is it about this KPMG role that interests you and makes you consider joining us?

Answer:

To be very transparent, the primary reason is the alignment between this role and my future career goals.

During my discussions with your team members, I got a clearer understanding of the Platform Security function and the responsibilities involved.

The role is closely related to the work I'm currently doing in DevSecOps, particularly security integration, vulnerability management, and security governance. At the same time, it offers an opportunity to take on broader responsibilities in cloud and platform security.

Another aspect that interests me is that the team is building the platform security capability from the ground up.

That would give me an opportunity to contribute to designing security standards, implementing security controls, and working with different engineering and support teams.

I'm particularly interested in growing from application-level DevSecOps security into a broader platform and cloud security engineering role.

That's why I see KPMG as a strong opportunity for my long-term career growth.
